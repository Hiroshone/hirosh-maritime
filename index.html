About Hirosh Maritime and Shipping Agency

Hirosh Maritime and Shipping Agency is a professional shipping agency and maritime services provider based in India. We provide shipping agency services, port agency support, vessel coordination, maritime documentation and logistics coordination for ship owners, vessel operators, charterers, freight forwarders and maritime partners. Headquartered in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, in close proximity to Vizhinjam International Seaport, we are strategically positioned to support shipping and maritime requirements while developing our service network across Indian ports. Our focus is on reliable coordination, responsive communication, efficient operational support and building long-term relationships with clients and maritime partners in India and internationally.
