About Hirosh Maritime and Shipping Agency

Hirosh Maritime and Shipping Agency is a shipping and maritime agency based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India. We provide professional shipping agency services, maritime support, vessel and port coordination, documentation assistance and logistics coordination. We are committed to providing reliable and responsive support to ship owners, operators, charterers, freight forwarders and maritime industry partners.
